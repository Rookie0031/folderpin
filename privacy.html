Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

FolderPin does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your Mac

  • The list of folders you pin, their original Date Added values, and the access permissions (security-scoped bookmarks) macOS gives FolderPin for those folders. These are stored only inside FolderPin's container on your Mac.
  • FolderPin reads and changes the Date Added of folders you choose. It does not read file contents.

Purchases

Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment. FolderPin only checks with StoreKit whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.
