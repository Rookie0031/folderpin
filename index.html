FolderPin Support

FolderPin keeps the folders you choose at the top of Finder lists sorted by Date Added, such as your Downloads folder. It lives in the menu bar.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: open an issue. We read every one.

How it works

When you pin a folder, FolderPin sets that folder's Date Added to the year 2100 using public macOS APIs. Finder then lists it first whenever the window is sorted by Date Added, newest first. FolderPin remembers the original date. When you unpin, the original date is restored exactly. Finder itself is not modified, and System Integrity Protection stays on.

Limits — please read

  • Pinned folders are on top only when Finder sorts by Date Added, newest first (View › Sort By › Date Added). Sorting by name, by Date Modified, or oldest first does not change.
  • When Finder groups items by date, pinned folders may appear in their own group at the top.
  • Other places that use Date Added also see 2100: Spotlight, Smart Folders that filter by Date Added, and the order of the Downloads stack in the Dock.
  • Folders in iCloud Drive (including Desktop & Documents when they sync with iCloud), on network volumes, or on disks that don't store Date Added can't be pinned.
  • If you move a pinned folder to another folder, macOS resets its Date Added. FolderPin pins it again the next time it starts or when you switch to Finder. If FolderPin loses access, the folder shows Allow Again.

Before you delete FolderPin

Open Settings and choose Unpin All and Restore Dates. If you delete the app first, pinned folders keep a Date Added of 2100.

Already deleted it? Reinstall FolderPin. If your pinned list is still there, choose Unpin All in Settings and the original dates come back. If the list is empty, Control-click each folder still dated 2100 in Finder and choose Unpin with FolderPin from Services. Its Date Added becomes the current time; the original date can't be recovered.

Common questions

Is Pro a subscription? No. Pro is a one-time purchase that lets you pin unlimited folders. The free version pins one folder. Unpinning and restoring dates is always free. Use "Restore Purchase" in Settings on another Mac signed in to the same Apple Account.

Can I pin from Finder? Yes. Control-click a folder and choose Pin to Top with FolderPin from Services.

Does FolderPin change my files? No file contents change. Only the Date Added of folders you pin.

Requires macOS 15 or later.
